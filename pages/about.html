About

As you may have heard by now, Rotur Assistant is the ultimate, all-in-one utility tool for Rotur, which takes almost full advantage of what's available via the Rotur API. It even allows for some things you can't do through official managers, such as managing account keys outside of Discord/OriginChats, or managing gifts you created.

The idea

The idea for Rotur Assistant came from a few things:

  • Rotur Toolbox: A service that was similar to what Rotur Assistant is, that was planned by Milodev123. Unfortunately, it never got far in development.
  • roturBOT: Prior to Rotur Assistant, the only way to modify your account keys was through roturBOT, and if roturBOT goes down, then you were basically out of luck.
  • OriginOS: As good and impressive as OriginOS is, it is sometimes not the most reliable option for interacting with Rotur, since clicks have a chance of sometimes not registering. For a while, OriginOS was the only way to interact with a good chunk of Rotur, like setting banners, managing Rmails, and even making ICN files. A workaround was discovered, where you tab out then tab back in to Origin, but that would grant you like 3 free clicks before you had to repeat the process. All in all, I feel like OriginOS is being held back by the fact that it's dependent on a scratch-based environment, and the fact that it's all running out of 1 sprite. In other words, OriginOS simply became too much for the Scratch engine to handle smoothly. Overtime, there have been several suggestions to have OriginOS rewritten in HTML/CSS/JS.

    As of recently, Mistium has recently considered rewriting origin in Preact/TS; that way, it can be faster and more stable, not being held back by any Scratch-related limitations. This is complemented by the fact that the primary version of OSL has switched to be Go-based, rather than JS-based (through a series of JS-injecting Scratch "patch" blocks). As of now, Mistium is currently testing the waters by replacing a good chunk of OriginOS's rendering to use HTML's rendering engine, rather than Scratch's rendering engine (and also tweaking Origin's OSL (OSL.js) to be more up-to-speed with OSL.go in terms of general syntax and logic).

All in all, Rotur Assistant was made to allow users to have more control over their Rotur experience, free of any client-imposed bugs or limitations.

History

The idea for Rotur Assistant started a few months before development on Rotur Assistant officially started. I had the idea of making a Discord bot called "Rotur Assistant", which would've been basically an upgraded version of roturBOT, but thankfully I decided to settle on making it a browser extension, since you can do a lot more within a browser extension environment than a Discord bot (It would've been impossible to add the universal account switcher if I went down the Discord bot route). The name "Rotur Assistant" was inspired by how the popular Discord bot, Dank Memer, has a helper bot named Dank Assistant, and was also inspired by the fact that Rotur Assistant was first envisioned as a Discord bot.

Shining a light

Some parts of Rotur, such as Items, Claw, and Rmail, were buried by obscurity, prior to Rotur Assistant. With Rotur Assistant providing interfaces for them, Rotur Assistant hopes to shine some light on those once-obscure parts of Rotur.

Development process

Development on Rotur Assistant started some time in late March. Since Rotur Assistant is my first full-fledged HTML/CSS/JS project, I learned some new things as I went along, such as new html tags like <details> and <section>. A good chunk of my HTML/CSS knowledge came from a college course I took about web design. I passed, though my general knowledge was still iffy. The web design course didn't cover JS at all, so I self-taught myself JS. I was already familiar with JS though, since I once made a Discord bot using node.js.

AI usage during development

I am proud to say, that most of the code for Rotur Assistant was hand-written. Claude AI was used to give the project a running start, and to teach me more about HTML/CSS/JS, but eventually, I learned enough to the point where I began working fully independent of AI. I still occasionally go back to it every now and then, especially when I need it to generate a function or calculation algorithm every once in a while. That or the occasional CSS error if I give up trying to debug it myself.

Rotur Impact

Overtime, some features that were found first or inspired/influenced by Rotur Assistant eventually made their way into Rotur itself. This includes:

  • User Lookup
  • Notifications Manager
  • Official Rmail Client
  • Official Claw Client

Some other details in official Rotur products, such as economy percentage in the official OriginChats ciient, were inspired by Rotur Assistant.
