Abandoned Features or Services

Origin-related or Rotur-related features that were worked on briefly, but then abandoned and likely forgotten about by Mistium. Features that are classified as finished won't be listed here.

Rotur Search

This was mainly created because Mistium was bored one time and wanted an excuse to create another repo under roturTW. Rotur Assistant offers its own version of Rotur Search, in the form of Lookup.

Rotur Pets

Mistium once entertained the idea of Rotur pets. At the time, it was thought that the idea was let go shortly after. However, during an analysis of all possible transaction types by Opencode to look for any transaction types the Wallet app didn't account for yet, two transaction types were discovered that suggest that Rotur pets were being developed at some point, then quietly abandoned shortly after.

Phosphorus (honorable mention)

Supposed to be an improved version of OriginOS's summit app, developed by Flufi. However, development has been pretty on-and-off with it, with Phosphorus being very rarely mentioned today.
