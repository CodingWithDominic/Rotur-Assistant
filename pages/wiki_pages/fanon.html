OriginOS Fanon

Overview

The originOS Fanon is a semi-unofficial wikipedia site for the Origin and Rotur community. While some articles on there are real, serious articles, the fanon is mainly filled with various memes and joke statements about notable users, random lore regarding events and users of the Rotur community, and others just signing their name. Anyone can contribute to it at anytime.

Migration off Fandom

Recently, a port was made to move the fanon wiki over to https://sophiewiki.mistium.com/
