The Rotur SMP

Server IP: mc.rotur.dev

Note: The last revision of this article was made during the setup stages of Season 2 of the Rotur SMP. As a result, the information about Season 2 may be inaccurate, and was written based on what I heard about it at the time.

Overview

The Rotur SMP is a Minecraft SMP that people of the Rotur community infrequently play on. The SMP is hosted by Milodev123, and it runs on Neoforge 1.21.1. The server has some quality-of-life commands to make players' lives easier. Like everything else mentioned in Rotur Economy, selling in-game items for Rotur credits can be a good income source, though the effectiveness of this method of making Rotur Credits depends on how active the SMP is.

History

The Origin SMP

Prior to the Rotur SMP, there was the origin SMP. The origin SMP only lasted for 5 days, since someone speedran and beat the game behind everyone else's backs.

Rotur SMP Season 1

Season 1 ran on Paper 1.21.1, with a notable mod being the Essentials mod. As a result of the events of the Origin SMP, the end was locked for a very long time when the Rotur SMP first started. Today, the end is unlocked, though the dragon fight itself is currently inaccessible due to barriers placed around the end fountain.

Rotur SMP Season 2

On July 25, 2026, 2 users, huopa and Andrew, decided to pioneer a movement for a modded season 2 of the Rotur SMP. They got tired of season 1 since it's been running for a little over a year at that point. Each user paid Milodev123 150 credits to further convince him to begin work on Season 2, with RattusDong pitching in 50 more credits, for a total of 350 credits as a community investment for Season 2 of the Rotur SMP. After some consideration, Milodev123 eventually agreed to set up Season 2.

How to join (Season 2)

Season 2 server IP: mc.rotur.dev

To join, go to https://mc.rotur.dev and link your Rotur account through there. An additional requirement is that at the time of linking, you must have Active (500+) or higher in the main OriginChats server. You don't need to maintain the role, you just need the role at the time you link your account. You can also ask to be whitelisted if you don't feel like grinding for Active (500+).

How to join (Season 1)

Season 1 server IP: s1.rotur.dev

The requirements in order to join this version of the Rotur SMP are vastly different compared to season 2's requirements. As of now, the only method of satisfying that requirement is to somehow find your way into the OriginOS Discord server (invites today are scarce, since as mentioned on the wiki regarding the Discord, the OriginOS Discord server is now a read-only server in favor of OriginChats), and then somehow get the "MINECRAFT!!!!" role. It used to be available through onboarding, but since onboarding was removed in the process of making the OriginOS server read-only, you're gonna have to ask someone who is still an admin in the Discord server to give you the role manually.
