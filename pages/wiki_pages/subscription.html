Overview

Rotur subscriptions are a way to support the Rotur project. In exchange, you receive a handful of benefits. A complete list of benefits, as well as how to obtain a Rotur Subscription, can be found at https://rotur.dev/membership.

Free

This is the subscription 98% of all Rotur accounts have. This is the default subscription for Rotur accounts when creating a Rotur account.

Lite

A small step-up from the free tier. Unlike other non-free tiers, this one costs 15 Rotur credits / month, and benefits include:

  • Bio Templating
  • Increased account storage from 1 GB to 5 GB
  • Less primitive access to roturBOT's AI on Discord or originChats (higher tiers have access to a better, more smarter model, while the free tier has access to a very basic model)
  • Access to Bio Templating
  • Appear in the settings page of originOS (and at the bottom of this page under "List of current subscribers"), and whatever else uses the https://api.rotur.dev/v2/supporters endpoint.
  • 400 character post limit on Claw (up from 300)
  • 20% discount on all paid cosmetics in the cosmetic shop.
  • More sign-in history remembered (10 -> 25)
  • A membership badge that evolves the higher your subscription is (though impossible to get the final badge tier unless you're Mistium)

Plus

The first of the two paid tiers. The price is £1.75 / month, and benefits include:

  • Access to all of the above in Lite, with some improvements
  • 20 GB of account storage space
  • Access to Sable, with a weekly usage limit.
  • Profile Notes on Rotur (Though Rotur Assistant provides its own notes system free of charge)
  • More URLs (20 -> 100) + custom URLs on https://gate.rotur.dev
  • Access to https://share.rotur.dev
  • Longer Bios (200 -> 500)
  • More transaction history (up to 1 year)
  • New bio templating option: Time
  • Animated profile pictures
  • Animated banners
  • 2× the daily credits you would normally receive
  • Max economy key increase from 5 to 20 (not to be confused with account keys)
  • More sign-in history remembered (10 -> 100)
  • 600 character post limit on Claw (up from 300)
  • Access to more features on Claw:
    • Polls
    • Post editing
    • Post scheduling
    • Post saving
    • More attachments per post (2 attachments, up from 1)
    • More pinned posts on your profile (3 pins, up from 1)
  • A crown next to your name on supported clients (example: Pounce)
  • 50 personal emojis on OriginChats
  • A supporter badge that showed you supported Rotur for 1 or more months. Unlike most other things on these lists, this is a thing you can keep after your subscription ends.
  • Increased sub-account limit (2 -> 10)

Drive

This tier has been discontinued. Any benefits it provided has been distributed into the Plus or Pro tiers (mostly Plus). Users who had this tier when it was discontinued were upgraded to the Pro tier.

Pro

Things continue to get more interesting and worthwhile. This is the second of the two paid tiers. The price is £5.75 / month, and benefits include:

  • Access to all of the above in Plus, with some improvements
  • 50 GB of account storage space
  • Unlock banners for free if you haven't yet
  • 3× the daily credits you would normally receive
  • All transaction history from all time is remembered
  • Max economy key increase from 5 to 50
  • More URLs (20 -> 1000) on https://gate.rotur.dev
  • New bio templating option: External URLs
  • Longer Bios (200 -> 1000)
  • 800 character post limit on Claw (up from 300)
  • Even more access to Claw's features:
    • Even more attachments per post (4 attachments, up from 1)
    • Even more pinned posts on your profile (5 pins, up from 1)
  • Increased Sable usage limits
  • 500 personal emojis on OriginChats
  • Increased sub-account limit (2 -> 50)

Max

This subscription is exclusive to Mistium, meaning it is currently unobtainable. While there's no official list of benefits, Rotur Assistant's estimated list of benefits include:

  • Access to all of the above in Pro, with some improvements
  • 200 GB of account storage space
  • Max economy key increase from 5 to 500
  • 1000 character post limit on Claw (up from 300)
